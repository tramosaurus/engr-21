## Problem 5.50
This one is tricky because you have two RL branches that are in parallel to each other.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1 -  Current through the switch</summary>

Note that after the switch is triggered at $t=0$, it becomes a wire. All the current will then flow through the wire.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2 - Finding current</summary>

Once the switch is triggered, you can treat each of the RL branches as separate circuits, since current leaving both those branches will go through the wire.
</details>
